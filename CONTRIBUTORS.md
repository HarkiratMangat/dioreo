## How credit works

- **You choose the name.** GitHub handle, display name, or anything reasonable — tell us in the pull request.
- **Credit appears in two places:** this file, and the `docs/CHANGELOG.md` entry for the release your work ships in.
- **Credit survives.** It stays even if the code is later refactored or replaced.
- **Removal is your call.** If a contribution is removed entirely, we'll remove the credit on request — or leave it, if you'd rather keep it.

Thanks to everyone who has taken the time to make this better.
