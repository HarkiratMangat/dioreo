DIOREO/PORTAL

Sign in with Discord

The portal is for Dioreo's admins. It uses your Discord account — there is no separate password to create, and none to lose.

Being signed in is not the same as being allowed. Every request re-checks your permissions server-side — the portal never trusts this browser about what you may see or do.
A session lasts 12 hours and lives in this browser only. Your staged work does not — it is held against your account, so signing out or losing this tab does not discard it, and signing back in returns you to it.
Dioreo reads only your Discord user ID. It requests no server list, no messages, and no email.
