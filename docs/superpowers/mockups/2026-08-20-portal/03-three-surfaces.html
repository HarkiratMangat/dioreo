Track

answers: when, and does it fit Season 7 · 14d left

The masthead. Nothing here is typed into — it is read, and dragged. Its job is to make the three defects that have no signal at all today impossible to miss: something running past the battle-pass end, two things overlapping that shouldn't, and a stretch of season with nothing scheduled. The staging rail below the divider is the existing draft area given a picture for the first time — that is what Promote to Live has never been able to show you before you pull it.

New here vs. screen 1: a zoom control (season / month / week), the draft rail as a second track under the divider, drag handles on band edges, patch notes as staged-or-live diamonds, and flags that name the fix rather than just the problem.

Manifest

answers: what exists, and change many at once 7 items · 2 selected

The working surface, docked under the Track. This is where the feature you originally filed lives — search narrows as you type, tick what you want, act on the selection. Discord cannot do this because a modal has no live channel and no checkbox; here it is just what a table does.

New here vs. screen 1: live search, filter chips, sortable columns, checkbox multi-select with a bulk bar that only appears when something is selected, one row shown mid-inline-edit, and state as a shape chip so it survives greyscale.

Board

answers: what am I changing, and what's blocking it 3 staged · 1 blocked

Re-jobbed. It is not a third view of your content — that was the redundancy. It is the changeset workspace: the pipeline a change moves along before it becomes real, with the tier-3 export requirement as a visible blocker in its own column rather than a dialog that ambushes you at commit. Every card carries its own inverse, so you can read what undoing it would do before you commit it.

New here vs. screen 1: columns are pipeline stages, not content states. Blocked is a real column with a stated reason and a one-click resolution. Commit is gated at the column boundary. And it is the only one of the three that works properly on a phone — columns become tabs.
