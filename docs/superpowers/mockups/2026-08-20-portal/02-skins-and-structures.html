Part 1 — three skins, identical structure

Only the tokens change: palette, type, radius, density. The markup and the shape rules are byte-identical in all three, so anything you notice is the skin. Every one uses colour for topic, shape for state — solid is live, hollow dashed is staged, hatched is a conflict.

Part 2 — three organizing structures, one skin

Same content, three mental models. Track organizes by when. Manifest organizes by what. Board organizes by what state. All rendered in skin A so you're comparing structure only.
