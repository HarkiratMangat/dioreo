Access

who can do what — and who is in here right now

Split out because it is the only page here that changes state. Its visual layer answers the question two directions: By admin is the grid you grant from; By scope answers "who can touch the calendar?" without reading across a row. Underneath, the manifest carries something the bot cannot know at all — live portal sessions, and the ability to end one. Revoking an admin in Discord doesn't kill a browser session; here it does.

Two things the grid does that the modal can't. It shows inheritance — a bare manage lights every page in a paler green, so you can see what you actually handed over instead of remembering that rule. Analytics

read-only — nothing here is re-derived

Your framing, and it is the right one: these numbers are fetched live from where they already live — AnalyticsRollup, AlertLog, ChangeLog, BootRecord and Cloud Monitoring, exactly the sources /bot analytics reads. There is no second computation to keep true, so the only maintenance is the same maintenance every page has: if you add a metric, you show it. The web adds two things a Discord embed cannot: history as a shape, and one action — revert.

Why the river is one table and not four. Alerts, changes and boots are all events, and they already live in four event-shaped collections. Filtering one stream beats switching between four lists, and it is the only way to see that an alert fired ninety seconds after a change was committed — which is the question you actually have when something breaks. The source column stays because both surfaces write through the same core: one history, two front doors.
