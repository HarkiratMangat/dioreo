## 6 · What can make this wrong

- The shared list class changes Compare's own list; Compare Empty C is re-shot after, and so is its weapon search.
- The category text in its hue can fail contrast for blue and purple; it takes the `--glo` lift, measured.
- Moving the form column's floor can put the fade under the side column's footer; only the form column moves.
- All as a topic chip may bring a dot or count style All never had; looked at on and off, both realms.
- Forced hover copies `:hover` rules and misses a hover declared on an ancestor; each hover is read on its own element.
