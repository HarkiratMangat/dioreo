/help

Every command you can use, listed inside Discord.

  • The cmd option jumps straight to one command instead of the whole list.
  • This is the only command that answers privately unless you say otherwise.
Try it/help
cmdoptionalsearchNot setJump straight to a specific command

Discord suggests matches as you type.

visibilityoptional2 choicesNot setShow this response only to you, or publicly to everyone in the chat. (Defaults to only you.)

Gunsmith

/gunsmith mp

The attachment build for one MP weapon, or every build in a class, the meta, or all of MP.

  • Discord searches as you type, and partial words match — "hol" finds Holger 26.
  • With nothing typed, the top of the list opens a whole set: All META builds, All MP builds, then each class.
  • The reply comes back in that weapon's category colour.
Try it/gunsmith mp
weaponrequiredsearch

An MP weapon, or All META / All MP builds to browse them

buildoptionalwhole numberNot setJump to a specific build number

A whole number, 1 or higher.

visibilityoptional2 choicesNot setShow this response only to you, or publicly to everyone in the chat.

/gunsmith dmz

The attachment build for one DMZ weapon, or the DMZ meta, or all of DMZ.

  • With nothing typed, the top of the list opens All META builds and All DMZ builds.
  • A meta browse has an MP Meta / DMZ Meta switch under it.
Try it/gunsmith dmz
weaponrequiredsearch

Gunsmith

/dmz

DMZ builds, which are a separate set from the MP ones.

  • DMZ builds are badged by combat range, Close or Mid-Long, rather than by weapon class.
  • Searching here never returns an MP build, and vice versa.
  • It suggests weapons only. To browse All META or All DMZ builds, use /gunsmith dmz.
Try it/dmz
weaponrequiredsearch

The DMZ weapon you want a build for

A whole number, 1 or higher.

Draws

/draw prices

What a draw costs, spin by spin.

  • Your region is the price of your first spin on a Mythic or Legendary Weapon draw: 10, 20 or 30 CP.
  • Every spin costs more than the last, and each draw lists every spin with the running total.
Try it/draw prices
typeoptional14 choicesNot setOpen one draw type on its own
regionoptional3 choicesNot setJump directly to a specific CP region
visibilityoptional2 choicesNot setShow this response only to you, or publicly to everyone in the chat.

Draws

/draw calculator

How much more CP you need, and the cheapest way to buy it.

  • It starts from the spins you have already done and what you hold: CP, Mythic Cards and Mythic Coins.
  • Bundle prices use the currency you set in /settings, and you can save up to 25 calculations.

Utilities

/colors

The colours in your own Discord profile, as hex values.

  • Reads your avatar, banner, display name, nameplate and decoration.
  • Animated avatars, banners, nameplates and decorations are sampled across the whole animation, not one frame.
  • In a server where you have a server profile it shows that one; source picks main or server.
Try it/colors
pageoptional5 choicesNot setJump straight to a specific color source.
sourceoptional2 choicesNot setRead colors from your main profile, or your profile for this server.

Utilities

/timestamp

A time that reads correctly for every person who sees it.

  • Plain English works — "tomorrow", "sun 4:30pm", "this saturday 7pm".
  • Leave style blank for all nine formats at once, or the style you saved in /settings.
  • Everyone who reads it sees it in their own timezone, not yours.
Try it/timestamp

e.g. "tomorrow", "sun 4:30pm", "19:30"
