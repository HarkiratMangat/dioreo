Gunsmiths

/gunsmiths search

The attachment build for one MP weapon.

  • Discord searches as you type, and partial words match — "hol" finds Holger 26.
  • A weapon with more than one build opens on the first; build jumps to another.
  • The reply comes back in that weapon's category colour.
/gunsmiths search
weaponrequiredsearch

The MP weapon you want a build for

Gunsmiths

/gunsmiths list

Every build in one category, the current meta, or all of them.

  • Eleven scopes: the seven weapon categories, all MP builds, the MP and DMZ meta picks, and all of DMZ.
  • The reply takes the colour of whichever scope you pick.

/timestamp

A time that reads correctly for every person who sees it.

  • Plain English works — "tomorrow", "sun 4:30pm", "in 2 hours".
  • Leave style blank and you get all nine formats at once.
  • Everyone who reads it sees it in their own timezone, not yours.
/timestamp
