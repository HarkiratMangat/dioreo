How credit works¶

You choose the name.

GitHub handle, display name, or anything reasonable — tell us in the pull request.

Credit appears in two places:

this file, and the docs/CHANGELOG.md entry for the release your work ships in.

Credit survives.

It stays even if the code is later refactored or replaced.

If a contribution is removed entirely

, we'll remove the credit on request — or leave it, if you'd rather keep it.
