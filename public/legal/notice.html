Portions of Dior's Builds — including source code, documentation, and these legal documents — were authored with the assistance of AI coding assistants, principally Claude (Anthropic PBC), used as a drafting and implementation tool under the continuous direction of the human author.

This disclosure is made deliberately, for three reasons.

6.1HONESTY.¶

Anyone reading, auditing, or contributing to this project is entitled to know how it was built.

6.2COPYRIGHT.¶

The enforceability of LICENSE depends on copyright subsisting in this work. The position in the United States, as set out by the Copyright Office and left undisturbed when the Supreme Court denied certiorari on 2 March 2026, is that:

  • material generated autonomously by AI, without human creative control, is NOT protected by copyright; but
  • a work is protected where a human exercised meaningful creative control over its expressive elements, and AI-assisted works are copyrightable to the extent of that human contribution.

Accordingly, the Licensor states affirmatively that throughout this project a human author (Harkirat Mangat) conceived the project and its features; specified requirements and behaviour; directed, reviewed, selected, rejected, corrected, and revised all generated output; determined the architecture, structure, sequence, and organisation of the codebase; and personally researched, selected, verified, arranged, and curated the game data compilations. The creative choices that give this work its expressive character are human choices.

Nothing in this disclosure is an admission that any part of the work lacks copyright protection, and no waiver of any right is intended or should be inferred.

6.3NO USER DATA IS SENT TO AI SYSTEMS.¶

AI assistance was used in *development*. It is not used to process end-user data at runtime. The only AI call in the running application is Google Vertex AI (Gemini) image-to-text extraction in the /autobuild command, which is restricted to the administrator's own Discord account and never receives end-user data, messages, or images. See PRIVACY.md §5.1.

LICENSE §4.7 separately prohibits YOU from using this project, its data, or its output to train or otherwise feed any AI or machine-learning system. That prohibition is unaffected by the Licensor's own use of AI tooling in development.
